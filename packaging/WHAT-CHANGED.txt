Opening, trading and closing
============================

The engine now moves money between users and events. An event still does not
know about users, and a user still does not know about events, so the engine
is the only place the two meet.

NEW
    InsufficientFundsException   an account was asked for more than it holds
    HoldingDto                   how many shares of an option a user holds
    UserStateDto, EventRoleDto   the users screen
    OpenReceipt, PayoutDto       results of opening and closing
    QuoteDto                     what a purchase would cost, before buying
    BuyDialog                    option, quantity, and a live price

CHANGED
    Account          withdraw refuses to go below zero, and canAfford asks
                     without throwing
    Event            records who holds which shares; open() takes the money
                     the market maker paid; buy() takes a user name;
                     close() pays the winners by holding and empties the account
    Transaction      carries the buyer's name
    CloseOutcome     carries what each winner is owed
    EventStateDto    carries the description, type, market maker, participations
                     and the opening cost
    engine           openEvent, buy and closeEvent all take a user name
    events screen    Open, Buy and Close buttons, enabled by who you are acting
                     as and by the state of the event
    top bar          an "Acting as" selector

THE RULES, AS THE EXERCISE STATES THEM
    Only the market maker of an event may open or close it.
    Opening an LMSR event moves the subsidy from his account to the event.
    Opening an order book event has him buy the initial stock, which is then
    his, at initial x d.
    He cannot open an event he cannot afford.
    An on-purchase commission is added to what the buyer pays and goes into
    the event account.
    Closing pays the winners from the event account by their holdings. A
    closing commission and anything left over both go to the market maker,
    and the event account ends empty.
    No user may go into a negative balance.

CHECKED ON PAPER
    Money is conserved. Opening, one purchase and a close on small.xml event 1
    starts with 10100 across the three users and ends with 10100.
    Same for a 20% on-close event: 15000 in, 15000 out.
